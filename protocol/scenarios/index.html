Observe without recording the conversation

RideWeave 0.2.1 persists bounded local diagnostics for rides, failed join attempts, and headset checks. A background writer records events, periodic counters, and device measurements. Ordinary records exclude speech and packet content. Shared reports also omit names, ride labels, invitations, keys, and raw network addresses.

Correlation pseudonyms support comparison across phones within a ride; they are not an anonymity guarantee against someone who already knows the source identities. Quota eviction can remove detail while retaining summaries. Recorder gaps and missing observations must remain explicit instead of being filled with invented values.

Keep evidence at its actual level

EvidenceWhat it can establish
Deterministic hostState transitions, authorization and ownership invariants
TLS/UDP loopbackReal socket framing and software duplex behavior
JVM / Android emulatorAdapter policy, JNI, UI and lifecycle behavior
Physical phones and helmetsRadio, motion, wind acoustics, coexistence, heat and battery: still requires qualification

A requirement mapped to a test is not itself a passing execution. Historical results apply to their recorded revisions. The documentation describes current implementation; cited software evidence does not qualify later radio behavior.

Deliberate boundaries

iOS, hotspot fallback, NAN Pairing interoperability, remote member eviction, and global authenticated termination are unimplemented. Moving-group range and reliability, screen-off survival, helmet acoustic quality, and battery duration are not qualified. Members share source keys. Parent coordination rejects known cycles but is not distributed consensus. DTX is optional and defaults off.

Authority: Scenario catalogue; diagnostics; human qualification. Public examples are synthetic. This guide is maintained against the private implementation; it does not imply access to its source.
